Yono Games Mela – Monthly Ranking: Official Rules

Last updated: 19 October 2026 · Organiser: Inferno, India · infernoss69@gmail.com

1. Free to enter

The monthly ranking ("Contest") is free. No purchase, payment or deposit is needed to take part or to win. Watching optional videos in the app does not give ranking points.

2. Who can take part

Anyone living in India can take part, except where prohibited by local law. Players under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission to claim a prize, and the prize is then paid to the parent or guardian. The organiser and their family members cannot win. Only one account per person.

3. Contest period

Each contest runs for one calendar month, from 00:00 UTC on the 1st day to 23:59:59 UTC on the last day. Points reset at the start of every month.

4. How points are earned

  • Ranking points come only from skill games (Coin Runner, Bubble Pop, Coin Magnet, Stack Tower, Speed Tap, Number Dash, Rummy) and the daily quiz.
  • Luck-based games (slots, plinko, fortune grid, lucky spin, scratch cards), coins and ads do not give ranking points.
  • A player can earn at most 3,000 ranking points per day.

5. Prizes

The 10 players with the most ranking points at the end of each month win: 1st ₹4,999 · 2nd ₹2,999 · 3rd ₹1,999 · 4th to 10th ₹999 each (paid by UPI or bank transfer). The current prize list is always shown in the app's Ranking screen; if prizes change, the app shows the new amounts before the month starts. Prizes are provided by the organiser, not by Google. Prizes cannot be exchanged or transferred. Any taxes on prizes are handled as required by law.

6. Claiming a prize

Winners must email infernoss69@gmail.com within 14 days of the month ending, quoting their Player ID (shown in the app's Profile screen). The organiser will then ask for the details needed to send the prize (for example a UPI ID) and may ask for identity verification. Prizes not claimed in time are forfeited.

7. Fair play

Using modified apps, bots, auto-clickers, multiple accounts or any other unfair method leads to disqualification. The organiser checks top scores before awarding prizes and may remove scores that look suspicious. The organiser's decision on winners is final.

8. Privacy

The ranking shows each player's nickname, avatar, Player ID and points. Winners' contact and payment details are used only to deliver prizes. See our Privacy Policy: https://infernoss69.github.io/privacy-policy.html.

9. Changes

The organiser may change or end the Contest at any time, with notice in the app. Changes do not affect prizes already won.

10. Google

Google is not a sponsor of, and is not involved in, this contest in any way.
