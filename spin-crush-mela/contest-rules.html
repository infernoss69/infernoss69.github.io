Spin Crush Mela – Monthly Ranking: Official Rules

Last updated: 19 October 2026 · Organiser: Inferno, India · infernoss69@gmail.com

1. Free to enter

The monthly ranking ("Contest") is free. No purchase, payment or deposit is needed to take part or to win. Watching optional videos in the app does not give ranking points.

2. Who can take part

Anyone living in India can take part, except where prohibited by local law. Players under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission to claim a prize, and the prize is then paid to the parent or guardian. The organiser and their family members cannot win. Only one account per person.

3. Contest period

Each contest runs for one calendar month, from 00:00 India time (IST) on the 1st to 23:59:59 IST on the last day of the month (the 30th or 31st; 28th or 29th in February). Points reset at the start of every month.

3a. Daily login requirement

To be eligible for a prize, a player must open the app (while connected to the internet) on every day of the contest month, India time. The app records each login day automatically and shows it in the Ranking screen. Days cannot be added later. Players who miss a day can keep playing and appear on the ranking, but cannot win a prize that month.

4. How points are earned

  • Ranking points come only from skill games (Candy Tap, Odd One Out, Target Rush, Bigger Wins, Arrow Flip, Pattern Pop, Speed Tap, Stack Tower) and the daily quiz.
  • Luck-based games (slots, plinko, lucky dice, find the gem, mystery boxes, lucky spin, scratch cards), coins and ads do not give ranking points.
  • A player can earn at most 3,000 ranking points per day.

5. Prizes

The 10 players with the most ranking points at the end of each month who also meet the daily login requirement win: 1st ₹4,999 · 2nd ₹2,999 · 3rd ₹1,999 · 4th to 10th ₹999 each (paid by UPI or bank transfer). The current prize list is always shown in the app's Ranking screen; if prizes change, the app shows the new amounts before the month starts. Prizes are provided by the organiser, not by Google. Prizes cannot be exchanged or transferred. Any taxes on prizes are handled as required by law.

6. Claiming a prize

Winners see a "You won" card in the app (Home and Ranking → Last month's winners) and must enter their name and UPI ID there within 14 days of the month ending (by the 14th, India time). Prizes are paid by UPI to that ID after scores are checked. The organiser may contact the winner at infernoss69@gmail.com for identity verification. A prize sent to the UPI ID the winner entered is treated as delivered. Prizes not claimed in time are forfeited.

7. Fair play

Using modified apps, bots, auto-clickers, multiple accounts or any other unfair method leads to disqualification. The organiser checks top scores before awarding prizes and may remove scores that look suspicious. The organiser's decision on winners is final.

8. Privacy

The ranking shows each player's nickname, avatar, Player ID and points. The ranking also shows how many days each player logged in. Winners' names and UPI IDs are visible only to the organiser and are used only to deliver prizes. See our Privacy Policy: https://infernoss69.github.io/spin-crush-mela/privacy-policy.html.

9. Changes

The organiser may change or end the Contest at any time, with notice in the app. Changes do not affect prizes already won.

10. Google

Google is not a sponsor of, and is not involved in, this contest in any way.
