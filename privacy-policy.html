Privacy Policy – Yono Games Mela

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains how Yono Games Mela ("the app"), published by Inferno, handles information.

Gameplay

The app is a collection of casual games played with virtual coins that have no real-world value. Coins cannot be bought, sold, withdrawn or exchanged for money or prizes. Your coins and game statistics are stored on your device. We do not collect your name, phone number, email or contacts.

Monthly ranking (Google Firebase)

To run the online ranking, the app signs you in anonymously with Google Firebase. You are not asked for your name, phone number or email. The app stores a random user ID, a random Player ID, the nickname and avatar you choose, and your monthly ranking points on Firebase servers. Other players can see your nickname, avatar, Player ID and points on the ranking. If you win a prize and contact us, we use the contact and payment details you send only to deliver your prize. See Firebase privacy information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app usage information. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. See also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Users in the European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form and can change their choice at any time from the "Privacy options" link on the home screen. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
